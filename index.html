GRID WATCHER

// LEARN QUANTUM ERROR CORRECTION BY PLAYING //

Quantum computers are fragile: their qubits glitch all the time. The fix is to spot errors without looking at the data — and undo them. Five short chapters, one idea each. Bring nothing but curiosity.

Chapter 6 will be based on D. Litinski, A Game of Surface Codes, Quantum 3, 128 (2019).
